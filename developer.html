Built on my own

SEQ Transit AI

Stop-level delay data for South East Queensland transport is not published anywhere, so I built the sensor first: an EC2 archiver polling the GTFS-Realtime feed every five minutes, giving me 156 million rows across 46 days. An XGBoost regressor on that archive reached a mean absolute error of 1.957 minutes against a 2.486 minute baseline. When it meets an unfamiliar stop, I force the confidence label to Low, because a wrong answer that looks confident is worse than no answer. Deployed on AWS (EC2, RDS, ECR).

Automated Scheme Research Tool

Government schemes go unused because the information sits in long PDFs nobody reads. I built a tool that ingests source URLs, summarises eligibility, benefits and application steps, and lets people ask questions of the material. LangChain for document loading, SentenceTransformers for embeddings, FAISS for similarity search, DistilBERT for question answering and a Streamlit chat interface. It runs on free Hugging Face models first, with OpenAI as an optional upgrade.

Maternal Health and Early Outbreak Warning

A prototype that screens maternal health risk factors and flags unusual spikes in reported community symptoms that might indicate an outbreak. I built it with logistic regression and z-score anomaly detection, because a health worker has to be able to understand why a case was flagged. I documented it as a prototype, not production software.
