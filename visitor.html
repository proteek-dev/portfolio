In short

At the City of Gold Coast I turn reporting requests from council departments into Power BI and Microsoft Fabric solutions, and I document the definitions and business rules so they survive after I move on. My AI work is hands-on: I have taken a BERT sentiment model and an OCR document-parsing system into production, and fine-tuned computer vision models that went into a live customer-facing application. That experience is why I'm comfortable saying when generative AI is the wrong tool: I've seen where it adds real value and where it quietly adds risk.
